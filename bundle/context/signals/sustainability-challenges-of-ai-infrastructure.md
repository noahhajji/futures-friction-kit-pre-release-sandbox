# Sustainability challenges of AI infrastructure

The expansion of AI infrastructure carries environmental consequences that cannot be ignored. Compute growth, data centre demand, cooling, water purification, material extraction and energy use all raise questions about the sustainability of AI infrastructure at scale.

## An uncertain footprint

- The future carbon and water footprint of AI remains highly uncertain. Affordable and capable low-carbon and water-efficient AI models may emerge, but the long-term sustainability of AI systems will depend on technical advances in chip design, computational engineering and data centre architecture, alongside laws, energy policy, and the state of public attitudes toward resource extraction and use.
- The issue becomes more complex in a world where AI regulation and the sustainability and climate agenda are developing along diverging paths.

## Sustainability moves upstream

- Sustainability will move upstream into infrastructure choices, model selection, procurement and responsible use.
- Decisions need to be made about how to match the resource intensity of infrastructure to the value created for the organisation, and whether more efficient approaches to AI use are being overlooked in the rush to gain short-term productivity increases.

## Across the horizons

Sustainability starts as a question of investing in efficiency, environmental technology, and measuring impact. Over time, solutions may emerge in the form of sustainable models, with trade-offs between computation power and resource intensity. In the longer term, sustainability concerns may end up reshaping which technological pathways are seen as legitimate at all in the wider economy.

## This includes

- Energy demand of AI
- Water intensity
- Supply chains for compute
- Sustainable digital infrastructure

## Questions

- How do we assess the environmental cost of our AI related infrastructure decisions rather than treating them as invisible backend choices?
- Where can smaller models, edge solutions, or more selective deployment create better balance between value and resource intensity?
- How should sustainability criteria influence procurement, partnerships, and technology architecture?
- What role can our organisation play in shaping more responsible norms for digital infrastructure and compute consumption?
- How do we communicate honestly about both the benefits and the environmental trade offs of AI adoption?
