# The futures cone

Everything beyond the present moment is the future. The cone is a conceptual tool that depicts the idea that there are multiple futures, that the future is not pre-defined, and that the further we move away from the present, the bigger the 'opportunity space' becomes.

## The zones

- Possible: future knowledge, what "may happen".
- Plausible: current knowledge, what "could happen".
- Probable: current trends, what is "likely to happen".
- Preferable: the desired future, a value judgement.

## Where foresight works

- Strategic foresight mainly turns its focus towards the plausible future. The probable is the territory of forecasting; what lies around it is foresight.
- Scenarios sit inside the cone as alternative futures. Wild cards sit outside it. Prediction is a single point.

## Figure: the futures cone

A horizontal arrow runs from the present on the left to the future on the right. From the present, lines fan out into a cone that ends in a large oval. Nested regions inside the oval show the successively narrower futures, possible, then plausible, then probable, with a small central dot labelled prediction and small circles inside the oval labelled scenarios; small dots outside the oval are labelled wild cards. Brackets on the right annotate the four zones, possible, plausible, probable and preferable.
