# Drivers of change / driving forces

The forces causing change by shaping and amplifying trends, increasing or decreasing their potential impact.

- Drivers are dynamic and interconnected, and include such things as shifts in attitudes and awareness, as well as economic and technological forces that influence how something spreads and grows.
- In essence, a driver is an underlying force that acts on something else, and consequently a trend can itself act as a driver when it exerts influence in other areas.
- In practice it is often impossible, and often unimportant, to distinguish between the two.
