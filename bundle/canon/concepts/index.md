# Concepts

- [The AI-mediated framework](/canon/concepts/ai-mediated-framework.md): when the person needs to see how AI now sits between people, information and organisations, layer by layer, how agents act in their own loop, or what mediation does to a news brand.
- [Assumptions](/canon/concepts/assumptions.md): when a plan or view of the future rests on beliefs nobody has tested or said aloud, and they need surfacing and questioning.
- [Berkana two-loop model](/canon/concepts/berkana-two-loop-model.md): when a new initiative or its champion is being marginalised by the dominant system, or the person needs to see which role they and their organisation play in a transition.
- [Cognitive biases in futures thinking](/canon/concepts/cognitive-biases-in-futures-thinking.md): when a team's reading of change looks coloured by attachment to the status quo, confirmation, expertise, recent events or consensus, and the bias needs naming.
- [Communicating foresight work](/canon/concepts/communicating-foresight-work.md): when foresight findings or scenarios must reach people who were not in the process, so that they move them, convince them and bear on a decision.
- [Complicated versus complex](/canon/concepts/complicated-versus-complex.md): when the person needs to judge whether a problem can be analysed to a right answer or must be explored, and so whether forecasting or foresight fits.
- [Constructive paranoia and no-regret moves](/canon/concepts/constructive-paranoia.md): when a leader asks what to do now when the organisation's future cannot be known, or a legacy newsroom is deciding what to change first and what to protect.
- [Drivers of change](/canon/concepts/drivers-of-change.md): when someone asks what is pushing a trend along, or how to tell a driving force from the trend it shapes.
- [Embedding foresight in the organisation](/canon/concepts/embedding-foresight.md): when the person wants foresight to take root in a busy organisation, or meets leadership resistance, inertia or a lack of time for the future.
- [Emergence](/canon/concepts/emergence.md): when someone asks why the future cannot be known in advance, or how complex systems keep producing genuine novelty.
- [Emerging technologies within a wider nexus](/canon/concepts/emerging-technologies-within-a-wider-nexus.md): when a technology question is being treated on its own and needs setting against the demographic, geopolitical, trust and lifestyle shifts it interacts with.
- [Existential innovation](/canon/concepts/existential-innovation.md): when a news organisation under pressure on its survival and identity must decide whether incremental change is enough or its core purpose needs rethinking.
- [First principles](/canon/concepts/first-principles.md): when a plan starts from today's format, distribution or metrics rather than from the medium's basic value assumption, or a brief already assumes the form of the answer.
- [Forecasting](/canon/concepts/forecasting.md): when someone wants a projection or prediction, or asks when extrapolating from past data can be trusted and when it gives a false sense of knowing.
- [Foresight set-up archetypes](/canon/concepts/foresight-set-up-archetypes.md): when the person is working out where foresight sits in their organisation, who carries it, and what that set-up can and cannot reach.
- [Four responses to public scepticism of AI](/canon/concepts/four-responses-to-ai-scepticism.md): when an organisation is choosing how much generative AI to use in its journalism and whether and how to tell its public.
- [From adoption to enablement](/canon/concepts/from-adoption-to-enablement.md): when a plan assumes a rolled-out AI tool will be used, starts from guidelines rather than value, or bypasses the middle managers who would have to integrate it.
- [From foresight to strategy](/canon/concepts/from-foresight-to-strategy.md): when foresight work is done and the person must turn it into strategic choices by testing current plans, deriving new options or probing with prototypes.
- [Future-preparedness and performance](/canon/concepts/future-preparedness-and-performance.md): when someone questions whether foresight pays off, or how much foresight an organisation actually needs for its environment.
- [Futures-driven innovation model](/canon/concepts/futures-driven-innovation-model.md): when an innovation or strategic choice is being made from the present alone, without the contexts and structures that shaped today's patterns or the possible futures that could change them.
- [Futures-driven innovation](/canon/concepts/futures-driven-innovation.md): when an innovation decision should start from where today's models will fall short, and be placed deliberately on a time horizon rather than all treated as near term.
- [Futures fallacies](/canon/concepts/futures-fallacies.md): when reasoning about the future leans on straight-line projection, holding all else equal, a fixed destination, prediction or personal exemption.
- [Futures friction](/canon/concepts/futures-friction.md): when an idea feels settled by a convincing answer, a plan is judged only by today's metrics, or a team wants to test what must remain true for an idea to make sense.
- [Futures literacy](/canon/concepts/futures-literacy.md): when someone asks what it means to be capable with the future, or how people learn to hold many futures in mind.
- [How to build a good scenario](/canon/concepts/how-to-build-a-good-scenario.md): when a scenario draft needs checking for whether it is plausible, consistent, distinct, memorable, challenging and useful to decision-makers.
- [Humanistic and scientific journalism](/canon/concepts/humanistic-and-scientific-journalism.md): when a plan sits between human voice and automated scale, or a newsroom cannot say which of its work is craft and which is system.
- [Liquid content](/canon/concepts/liquid-content.md): when the person is weighing content that adapts to each user and context against fixed formats, and what that shift does to where value lies.
- [Liquid foresight](/canon/concepts/liquid-foresight.md): when someone asks how foresight itself could become a continuous, AI-supported capability inside the organisation rather than a one-off deliverable.
- [Use the liquid wisely or choose human first](/canon/concepts/liquid-or-human-first.md): when a medium must choose between personalised, AI-adapted content and content carried by clear human voices, or risks drifting into the half-hearted middle.
- [Megatrends](/canon/concepts/megatrends.md): when someone asks what counts as a megatrend, how it differs from trends and weak signals, or which long-term forces can be treated as relatively certain.
- [Organisational preparedness](/canon/concepts/organisational-preparedness.md): when the person asks whether their organisation is ready for change beyond the technology, or needs to tell incremental from radical innovation.
- [Reperception](/canon/concepts/reperception.md): when someone asks what foresight is ultimately for, or how scenarios shift people's sense of what the future could be.
- [Rewiring starts in our heads](/canon/concepts/rewiring-starts-in-our-heads.md): when a plan assumes people can question assumptions on top of daily work, or an idea was dismissed before anyone asked what the user needed.
- [Scenarios](/canon/concepts/scenarios.md): when someone asks what a scenario is and what it is for, or treats scenarios as forecasts to be judged right or wrong.
- [Scoping a foresight project](/canon/concepts/scoping-a-foresight-project.md): when a foresight project is being set up and its purpose, the decisions it should influence, realistic expectations or who to involve are not yet settled.
- [Second brains](/canon/concepts/second-brains.md): when an organisation wants to learn from what others have tried and why, or is deciding who controls shared knowledge, what can be reused and which AI systems may access it.
- [Service, not publication](/canon/concepts/service-not-publication.md): when a plan's responsibility ends at publication, measures stories produced, or begins and ends with a box where someone types a question.
- [Strategic foresight fallacies](/canon/concepts/strategic-foresight-fallacies.md): when an organisation seeks certainty from foresight, squeezes it into planning routines, or expects it to hand over a strategy by itself.
- [Strategic foresight](/canon/concepts/strategic-foresight.md): when someone asks what strategic foresight is and is not, or how it should feed strategy rather than predict.
- [The agentic information economy](/canon/concepts/the-agentic-information-economy.md): when a plan assumes articles read by people remain the unit of value, or asks what journalism is for when answers reach people through agents.
- [The CIFS foresight process](/canon/concepts/the-cifs-foresight-process.md): when the person needs to see where a piece of foresight work sits in the arc from scoping through uncertainties and scenarios to strategic action.
- [The friction field](/canon/concepts/the-friction-field.md): when a trend looks set to continue and the person needs to weigh the forces pushing it against those that slow, stop or divert it, including timing.
- [The futures cone](/canon/concepts/the-futures-cone.md): when someone confuses probable with plausible futures, or asks where foresight, forecasting, scenarios and wild cards sit in the space of futures.
- [The human-centred newsroom](/canon/concepts/the-human-centred-newsroom.md): when a plan competes on speed and volume, measures output, or recruits for shifts rather than for voice and relationships.
- [The Scoping Circle](/canon/concepts/the-scoping-circle.md): when stakeholders need to agree a foresight project's focal question, context, decisions to influence, approach and research before the work starts.
- [The story that acts](/canon/concepts/the-story-that-acts.md): when a plan optimises for attention or for intention so completely that it closes the space for judgement, surprise and stopping.
- [The three horizons of AI-mediated transformation](/canon/concepts/three-horizons-of-ai-mediated-transformation.md): when an organisation's AI work is mostly efficiency gains and the person needs to see what reorganising workflows and reshaping business models would demand.
- [Three information ecologies](/canon/concepts/three-information-ecologies.md): when a plan needs placing in the information value chain, or treats AI as another distribution shift rather than a reorganisation of how information is produced, maintained, discovered and used.
- [Three publisher positions](/canon/concepts/three-publisher-positions.md): when a publisher is deciding where it creates and captures value as AI systems mediate between its journalism and the people and organisations that use it.
- [Trends](/canon/concepts/trends.md): when someone asks what a trend is, how firmly it is grounded, or whether an observed shift will simply carry on.
- [True truth as gold](/canon/concepts/truth-as-gold.md): when the person is deciding how a news medium can make verified truth valuable, and worth paying for, as AI-made content floods and personalises information.
- [Uncertainty](/canon/concepts/uncertainty.md): when the person needs to tell risk from uncertainty, or find which high-impact, unresolved forces a plan must be prepared for.
- [Weak signals](/canon/concepts/weak-signals.md): when someone has spotted a faint early development at the fringes and needs to judge whether it could grow into a trend or fade away.
- [What tools to use when?](/canon/concepts/what-tools-to-use-when.md): when the person must choose or combine foresight tools for a given purpose and audience, pitched to challenge without seeming implausible.
- [Wild cards](/canon/concepts/wild-cards.md): when the conversation turns to low-probability, high-impact surprises that a plan cannot forecast but should be able to absorb.
- [Worldview](/canon/concepts/worldview.md): when a view of the future seems bound by an unexamined frame from the present, or one official future is crowding out others.
