# Strategic foresight seeks to challenge mental models and organisational perspectives

## What to challenge

- Our tendency to favour the 'business as usual' future.
- Our tendency not to adequately explore viable alternatives.

## What to refresh

- Old and obsolete imagery of the future, so often held onto by organisations.

## Why

- For the betterment of decisions that are more informed.
- For decisions less clouded by judgemental biases and misguided assumptions.
