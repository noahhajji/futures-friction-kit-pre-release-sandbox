# Strategic foresight looks beyond the traditional strategic planning time horizon

## The principle

- Strategic foresight most often deals with the medium to long-term future.

## The current planning horizon

- The current strategic planning time horizon is three to five years.
- Thinking within this horizon tends to limit 'mental flexibility'.
- It often leads to path-dependent thinking.

## Thinking further out

- Thinking ten or more years out better enables the exploration of viable future alternatives.
- These alternatives may be significantly different from today.
