# Strategic foresight is a systems-thinking approach to support resilient organisational futures

## The principle

- In a time where forecasts and projections are not enough, strategic foresight aims to achieve a genuinely high-quality view of potential future outcomes.
- Strategic foresight focuses on potential changes in an organisation's external environment.

## What this includes

- Outcomes 'beyond the numbers'.
- Potentially impactful signals that would otherwise be disregarded.

## Why it matters

- These changes influence the strategic environment.
- The strategic environment in turn shapes strategic decisions.
