# Strategic foresight explores the future before considering implications for the present

## The principle

- Strategic foresight focuses on exploring the future before considering implications for the present.

## The direction of learning

- We are trying to learn about the present through the lens of the future.
- This is in contrast to trying to understand the future from the perspective of the present.
