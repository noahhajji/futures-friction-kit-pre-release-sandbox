# Scenario immersion

Scenario immersion is the step from foresight to strategic insights. Having built a set of scenarios, the organisation immerses itself in one future at a time to understand what it would mean for its users, its competitive position and its strategic direction. It pulls on elements from strategy tools frequently used in strategic planning, such as the value proposition canvas and industry dynamics analysis.

## How it works

- Work one scenario at a time, from inside it.
- Consumer or user needs and expectations: what are they in this scenario, and how are they different from today?
- Competitive landscape: what does the playing field look like? Who are the players, what are the market characteristics, how has the field changed?
- Strategic implications: which future outcomes must be strategically considered, because they may impact strategic direction, objectives or actions? Identify fewer clear implications rather than many unclear ones.

A strategic implication is a future outcome that must be strategically considered, as it may impact strategic direction, objectives or actions.

## Which tools, for which insight

Depending on the sought-after strategic insight, different tools can be brought into the immersion.

Understanding tomorrow's options:

- Value proposition, or jobs to be done: what is our future value proposition, and what solution will fit our users and markets in this scenario?
- Business model canvas: what does the future competitive landscape look like, and what business model will fit our solution in this scenario?
- Industry dynamics analysis, personas and customer journeys, segment analysis, value-chain analysis.

Identifying today's decisions:

- SWOT-TOWS: how will we use our strengths and overcome our weaknesses to grasp the future opportunities and mitigate the future threats in this scenario?
- Wind-tunnelling: will our current strategies and initiatives still provide a path to success, and what appears successful across scenarios?
- Must-win battles: what are the key things we need to do to win in this scenario?

## Key things to consider

- Keep the scope of an immersion explicit, for example implications related to consumers, competition and market only, so the discussion does not sprawl.
- The immersion is not yet strategy. It explores a plausible future that should inform strategy.
