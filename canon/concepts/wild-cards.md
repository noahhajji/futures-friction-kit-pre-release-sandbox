# Wild cards

Events or developments with low (perceived) probability but high impact when they occur.

- Wild cards have the potential to significantly alter the future landscape in unpredictable ways, by disrupting existing realities and developments, making them critical considerations for strategic foresight.
- Although wild cards cannot fully be anticipated or planned for, wild card analysis helps organisations better adapt to disruptive surprises by considering possible wild cards and assessing their potential implications.
- The term was co-introduced by CIFS in 1992.
