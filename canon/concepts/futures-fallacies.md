# Futures fallacies

Fallacies are failures in our reasoning that lead to mistaken beliefs. Futures fallacies are the detrimental thinking patterns that appear when we think about the future.

- Linear projection: presuming that future change will be a simple and steady extension of past and current trends.
- All else unchanged: considering only a few aspects of change while holding everything else equal.
- The arrival: envisioning possible futures as static objects, such as a destination or a goal.
- The prediction, or planning: believing it is possible to predict or plan the future.
- Future personal exemption: being overly optimistic about one's own future despite a realistic or even dystopian take on our collective futures.
