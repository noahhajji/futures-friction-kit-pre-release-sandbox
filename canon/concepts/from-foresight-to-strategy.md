# From foresight to strategy

Having done the foresight, how does it link to the strategic choices about to be made? There are three bridges, and in practice organisations blend them.

## Strategy-forward: scenario-tested strategy

- Most, if not all, organisations already have a strategy and a portfolio of initiatives and responses. Start with the existing strategy, initiatives, products, services, projects and risk responses, and test their merits against the set of scenarios, to see whether they still provide a path to success. This is wind-tunnelling.

## Future-back: scenario-led strategy

- Put existing strategies to one side and start with the scenarios, using the picture of tomorrow as a resource for stimulating inspiration and imagination and for bridge-thinking. What solutions does this future indicate? What different or altogether new strategies are suggested here? From the scenarios, develop strategic options, initiatives, innovation ideas and risk responses.

## Strategic probing

- A newer approach that has come in from the product and service design professions. Test strategies and initiatives in the direction the foresight suggests, prototyping different solutions rapidly, and inject that learning back into the foresight process to create experimental proof.
