# Futures literacy

The capability to imagine, question and use the future as a resource for better understanding the present and acting with intention.

- Developed within UNESCO as a core competency for the 21st century, it treats engaging with the future as something that can be learned and strengthened, much like reading and writing, and it enhances our ability to prepare, recover and invent in the face of change.
- A futures-literate person is able to hold many possible futures in mind, rather than a single expected one, and to draw on them to see the present differently, becoming more comfortable with uncertainty, novelty and emergence.

## Future as a verb

- Future as a noun means the distant time yet to come. Future as a verb means actions and processes, 'futuring', that anticipate and influence future outcomes in the present. Changing the term from noun to verb describes using the future to inform present-day decisions.
