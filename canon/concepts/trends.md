# Trends

Observable and/or measurable directional shifts in a situation or in the way people behave. They can vary in strength, direction (increasing, decreasing or stable) and impact.

## How a trend is represented

- Numerically, through indicators that can be measured and supported by data.
- Non-numerically, as a perceived development that may only partially be supported by data, often understood through examples or observations.
- In practice, most trends carry elements of both.

## How trends behave

- Trends shaping the future are interconnected and interdependent, with different time horizons, reach and levels of impact.
- They often blend into each other, making them difficult to separate.
- There is no guarantee that a trend observed in the past will continue unchanged in the future.
- Countertrends often emerge in response or opposition to the dominant one.
