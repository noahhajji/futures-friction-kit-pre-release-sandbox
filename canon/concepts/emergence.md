# Emergence

A feature of complex systems, like societies.

- New patterns, behaviours or properties arise from the interactions within the system and could not have been inferred from the individual parts alone.
- Emergence is a source of genuine novelty, and a central reason the future cannot be known in advance, only anticipated, because complex systems continually produce what was not there before.
