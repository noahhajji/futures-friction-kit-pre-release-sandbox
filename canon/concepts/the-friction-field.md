# The friction field

The way future change occurs depends on the outcome of the 'power struggle' between favouring and limiting forces. New trends and developments create friction in the existing environment, and the future unfolds in the friction field.

## The forces

- Change drivers and enablers are the forces that cause a trend to move, and the factors that promote or catalyse a driver. A change driver may itself be a trend.
- Change turners and blockers are the forces that oppose a trend by delaying, stopping or diverting it, and the factors that cause resistance to change.
- Change enters the field on one side; implications for societies, industries and the business environment emerge on the other.

## Reading the field

- Do not take a trend at face value. A trend can relatively easily be identified and analysed, but understanding the forces and factors that either promote or limit it gives the more detailed picture.
- Does the development challenge prevailing social, cultural or ethical norms? Then change is likely to proceed slowly, if at all.
- Whose side is the law on? The law is almost always, by design, a drag on the future, for good and for bad.
- Very often it is about timing.
