# Foresight and strategy are complementary parts of one process

## The principle

- Foresight and strategy are complementary parts of one process in pursuit of future organisational success.
- Foresight should always be connected to strategic purpose.

## Without foresight

- Strategy easily becomes blind to contextual change.
- Strategy becomes a recipe for failure when change occurs.

## Without strategy

- Foresight easily becomes conjecture.
- Foresight becomes disconnected from organisational reality.

## The relationship

- Foresight outcomes are not strategies in themselves.
- Foresight outcomes should inspire insights for strategic planning.
