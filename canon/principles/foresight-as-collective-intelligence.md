# Strategic foresight should be approached as a form of collective intelligence

## The principle

- Strategic foresight should be seen and approached as a form of collective intelligence.

## How quality outcomes arise

- Quality strategic foresight outcomes can only be generated and assessed through dialogue and exchanges between people.
- Those people bring diverse perspectives to the table, as non-partisan as possible.
- This is key to facilitating new, viable perspectives concerning future developments of relevance.

## What does not work

- It is not possible to passively 'study the future' and hope to learn much worthwhile.
