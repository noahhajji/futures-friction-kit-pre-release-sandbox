# The strategic foresight process is at least as important as the final outcomes

## The principle

- In strategic foresight, the future is not a destination; it is a tool.

## What the process should do

- Develop learning.
- Broaden participants' horizons.
- Help build a shared understanding of potential future outcomes.

## The hardest task

- The most difficult task when working with strategic foresight is to facilitate the transition of helping people think of the future in new ways.
