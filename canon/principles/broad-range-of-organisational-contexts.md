# Strategic foresight can be applied to a broad range of organisational contexts

## Contexts within the organisation

- Better strategic and policy anticipation, to identify and prepare sooner for future opportunities and risks.
- Business or policy innovation, to spur new thinking.
- Future-proofing, to identify gaps and stress-test existing or proposed strategies against a range of plausible futures.

## Contexts beyond the organisation

- Context can be extended beyond the organisation to inspire thought-leadership or visioning.
