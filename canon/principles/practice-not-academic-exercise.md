# Applying strategic foresight in practice is not an academic exercise

## The academic foundation

- Foresight is a well-established field with a robust academic underpinning.
- It adopts principles of good scientific practice.

## The practice

- Successful strategic foresight is pragmatic and hands-on.

## What the practice relies on

- Rigorous and structured methodologies.
- The fine art of sense-making, intuition, curiosity and creativity.
