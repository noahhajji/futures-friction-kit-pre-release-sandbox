# Strategic foresight explores plausible futures rather than making predictions

## The principle

- Strategic foresight is not about making predictions of the future, but rather exploring plausible futures.

## Why prediction fails

- The future is inherently unpredictable.
- Many factors may come together in complex ways to create surprising futures in a non-linear world.
- There are no definitive answers about what the future will hold.

## What informs plausible futures

- Trajectories and external sources of change.
- Emergent signals of change.
- Emerging critical uncertainties.
