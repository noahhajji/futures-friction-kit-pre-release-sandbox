# The zero-click era

The use of AI products such as chatbots to get knowledge and information has exploded in recent years. To some extent it already replaces functions that news media could previously fill, especially among the young, of whom sixteen per cent use AI for news. At the same time AI-generated search results and similar products fundamentally change how we find and use information: a movement away from search and links towards a single interface where we get answers to our questions.

The so-called zero-click era already appears readable in drastically falling traffic to news media. In the United States, half of the fifty largest news sites lost a fifth of their traffic in the year to April 2026. Some lost half or more, and dominant national and international news brands also bled traffic.

Figure: a bubble chart of the fifty largest news sites, showing year-on-year percentage change in web traffic to April 2026, with bubble size as monthly visits. A few sites appear as growing bubbles at the far left, one at around plus one hundred and fifty per cent and a newsletter platform at around plus twenty-five per cent; the rest of the field is dominated by falling bubbles following a downward trend line towards about minus thirty per cent, with the marked national and international brands in the range of about minus five to minus twenty per cent.

The development points to the structure of the internet being under radical change.

## Wider effects

- AI-generated overviews make errors in roughly one answer in ten, according to one analysis.
- Far fewer people visit a national public health information site than a year earlier, because they get their answers directly from AI, so an information crisis can become a health crisis.

## What it means for news media

Building community is also about the less lofty task of building a direct relationship to the user: being a destination for users and building a direct relation, rather than merely becoming a supplier of content for others. Results have been achieved by working from the assumption of receiving zero search traffic, so that everything comes to depend on building a direct relationship. Without traffic from outside, the question has to be asked very directly: why should people come to you?
