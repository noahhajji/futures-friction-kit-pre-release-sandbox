# The rise of agentic organisations

Agentic AI is, in a basic explanation, AI that can pursue goals through actions, not just generate responses. In 2026, a visible shift is starting where agents not only perform assigned tasks, but also interact with other AI agents in ways that open up new paths not fully anticipated in the original prompt. This shapes how work is created, coordinated and executed, and points towards the emergence of agentic organisations, where AI becomes embedded within operational systems rather than sitting at the edge of them.

## What is already happening

- Agents have already begun to take on substantial roles to optimise workflows. This changes expectations around speed, accountability and collaboration. The release of OpenClaw has brought about widespread concerns regarding the safety of highly autonomous agentic AI assistants, but also highlights the potential of these technologies to transform personal workflows as they increase in maturity.
- Interfaces that translate intent directly into workflow systems allow individuals and teams to build, test and deploy applications at unprecedented speed. As the cost and friction of building software decline, traditional software models may shift, with the possibility that some forms of software as a service capture less value in the value chain as capabilities become more embedded, modular and agent driven, also known as the SaaScopolypse.
- Together these developments suggest that organisations increasingly operate as dynamic systems of humans and AI agents working in collaboration. The important question is how they reshape coordination, knowledge flows and decision making inside organisations all together.

## What leaders will need to deal with

- Where does human judgement remain essential, where can agentic systems create leverage, and how are trust, accountability and organisational values maintained when actions are partially automated?
- At a deeper human level, whether organisations need to reintroduce intentional cognitive friction into their processes, so the removal of friction does not slide into overdependence or, eventually, a significant loss of control over the agentic workforce.

## Across the horizons

Agentic systems initially enhance existing tasks and workflows. Over time they begin to reorganise collaboration and decision making across teams. In the longer term, they may reshape the very structure of organisations, as agency and responsibility are increasingly shared between people and intelligent systems.

## This includes

- Agentic AI
- AI-mediated workflows
- Hybrid human and AI teams

## Questions

- Which parts of our organisation are ready for agent supported workflows, and which require stronger human judgement or accountability?
- How should roles, responsibilities, and escalation paths change when AI agents begin to act across multiple functions?
- What forms of oversight, logging, and transparency are needed to keep agentic systems aligned with our values and risk thresholds?
- How can we build organisational trust in human and AI collaboration without creating complacency or anthropomorphising the systems?
- What new aptitudes will leaders need if the organisation increasingly operates through a mix of human judgement and machine-initiated actions?
