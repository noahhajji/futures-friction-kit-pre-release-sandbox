# The great information repricing

Much suggests that clear usefulness is essential when it comes to monetising truth in these years. Profit margins are markedly larger on selling intelligence and solutions that companies and people can use very directly than on classic journalism. Value is migrating from the content layer to the intelligence layer.

Figure: a scatter plot of revenue per employee against the share of revenue from intelligence and data products. Classic news media sit bottom left with near-zero margins and a consumer news subscription as their peak product; data and intelligence companies rise up and to the right along a dashed "repricing curve", with margins above thirty-five per cent and an institutional terminal as their peak product. The gap is described as a pricing ratio of 275 to 1 for the exact same raw material.

## The ecosystem taxonomy

| | The intelligence business | The attention aggregator | The public good |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product | Reduced uncertainty for decisions | Awareness of past events | Civic accountability |
| Distribution | Embedded in professional workflow | Social and search feeds | Public access |
| Economics | High margin, extreme pricing power | Structural collapse, as AI makes awareness free | Non-market, requires philanthropic or grant funding |

## What it means for news media

Put simply, there is money in the solution economy. But ordinary journalism, which one cannot necessarily use very directly in one's life, may rather have to be understood as a public good. The development poses new challenges to financing, and perhaps to the need for support, and possibly calls for news media to focus at least parts of their offering into a solution economy. Otherwise it will, as now, largely not be media but domain experts and others who build that type of information business.

A major report on the new AI landscape does not mention news media as a specific category at all, only as infrastructure. If content becomes infrastructure, verification becomes the product. Can news media develop formats for that within a new solution and intelligence economy, so that truth actually becomes gold?
