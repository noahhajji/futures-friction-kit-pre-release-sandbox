# Signals

- [AI moves into the physical world](/context/signals/ai-moves-into-the-physical-world.md): when a plan for physical operations such as printing, distribution, events or studios assumes automation stays confined to fixed machinery away from people.
- [Attention splinters](/context/signals/attention-splinters.md): when a plan relies on a shared front page, mass reach or engagement metrics while audiences move into personalised AI feeds that know them but stay opaque to them.
- [Audiences move to creators](/context/signals/audiences-move-to-creators.md): when a plan to reach younger audiences assumes they will come to the brand's own channels and trust institutional objectivity rather than creators and personalities.
- [Convenience over accuracy](/context/signals/convenience-over-accuracy.md): when a plan or paywall rests on audiences valuing precise, verified news enough to choose or pay for it over approximately right AI answers.
- [The cyber security of AI-mediated infrastructures](/context/signals/cyber-security-of-ai-mediated-infrastructures.md): when a plan connects AI to archives, workflows, email or publishing systems, or treats AI security as an IT matter rather than a risk to trust and operations.
- [From static to liquid](/context/signals/from-static-to-liquid.md): when a plan invests in fixed articles, formats or interfaces that win the click, while answers are increasingly assembled in real time by AI systems.
- [The geopolitics of tech](/context/signals/geopolitics-of-tech.md): when a plan depends on foreign cloud, AI models or platforms and treats supplier choice as a cost question rather than a strategic dependency.
- [Machine audiences](/context/signals/machine-audiences.md): when a plan assumes the audience is human readers, while a growing share of traffic is AI agents that want structured data and clear context rather than narrative.
- [Machines as the primary audience](/context/signals/machines-as-the-primary-audience.md): when a plan assumes its journalism will be read by people on the newsroom's own pages, or that the article stays the unit of information and of value.
- [Neurotechnology and the cognitive dimensions of AI](/context/signals/neurotechnology-and-the-cognitive-dimensions-of-ai.md): when a plan hands research, editing or judgement to AI, or builds audience products that do the thinking for users, without asking which cognitive skills are lost.
- [Newsrooms start to explain their AI choices](/context/signals/newsrooms-start-to-explain-their-ai-choices.md): when a plan uses AI in the journalism without deciding whether and how to tell the public, or assumes that nobody explains their AI choices.
- [Platforms move from distribution to content](/context/signals/platforms-move-from-distribution-to-content.md): when a plan depends on platform or AI distribution, licensing deals or a direct relationship with users that the tech companies owning the AI entry points could take over.
- [Public scepticism of AI in news](/context/signals/public-scepticism-of-ai-in-news.md): when a plan assumes the audience will welcome AI in the journalism, will not notice it, or will credit a newsroom for not using it.
- [Quantum – the next era](/context/signals/quantum-the-next-era.md): when a long-range technology, security or data plan leaves out shifts in computing that could break encryption or change what can be modelled, and no one owns watching them.
- [The reshuffling of ecosystems](/context/signals/reshuffling-of-ecosystems.md): when a plan defends the product or content the organisation makes while value moves to whoever interprets intent, coordinates data and owns the trusted layer.
- [The rise of agentic organisations](/context/signals/rise-of-agentic-organisations.md): when a plan brings AI agents into editorial or business workflows without settling who decides, who is accountable and where human judgement stays.
- [Simulations on the rise](/context/signals/simulations-on-the-rise.md): when a plan or decision could be rehearsed before launch, for instance by testing audience or advertiser responses with simulations, synthetic data or digital twins.
- [Sustainability challenges of AI infrastructure](/context/signals/sustainability-challenges-of-ai-infrastructure.md): when a plan scales AI use, compute or data infrastructure without weighing its energy, water and climate costs or how audiences and regulators will judge them.
- [The great information repricing](/context/signals/the-great-information-repricing.md): when a business model counts on selling general news awareness, while value moves to intelligence that reduces uncertainty for decisions and to grant-funded civic journalism.
- [The knowledge freezer](/context/signals/the-knowledge-freezer.md): when a plan assumes AI companies can keep drawing on journalism while newsrooms shrink, or weighs licensing content to AI without asking what happens when fewer stories are made.
- [The zero-click era](/context/signals/the-zero-click-era.md): when a plan relies on search or social referrals still delivering audience when it pays off, rather than on a direct relationship with users who choose to come.
