# Machine audiences

A particular point in the relationship between media and the AI entry points concerns whom media produce content for: human users or a machine audience. Traffic from the latter takes up more and more. The customer or reader is agents that the medium's information passes through. That challenges the business model for publishers when the audience is the machine, and it requires commercial innovation. News media are geared to produce artefacts, not data for agents.

## What it challenges

- News media have to move higher up the value chain. Otherwise they will struggle with what has been called the largest cultural shift ever for news organisations, against which digitisation was a piece of cake.
- When the customer is an agent, some classic journalistic forms become less valuable. A journalistic narrative of two thousand words is not optimal for AI agents; a list and clear context are more usable. A narrative can still create good value for people, and support what can be called community journalism.
- Many media are not conscious enough of the two types of content and the two types of user, and they do not use two rigorous approaches.

## What it calls for

As with the choice between liquid and human-first content, it is about choosing very clearly, or having very great awareness of when you make what. Some media have begun to make two kinds of content: one for machines, for example with lists, in front of the paywall, and another kind for people behind the paywall.
