# The knowledge freezer

AI platforms increasingly rely on trusted news content to understand and respond to real-time events, which indicates that the AI companies need some of the journalistic content. Agreements giving AI assistants access to news in real time have been seen as a structural shift in the AI ecosystem.

A darker version of that relationship of dependence was mapped in 2026 under the heading "the knowledge freezer". The risk is that fewer journalistic stories are made, because news media are pressed by AI. Over time AI can therefore not draw on as many news stories within certain fields, so the quality of AI also falls, in a self-reinforcing negative loop.

## What it challenges

- The dependence runs both ways: AI needs the journalistic content it is displacing.
- A fall in the volume of journalism in a field does not only hurt the media producing it; it degrades every AI answer that relies on that field.
