# Sector

- [AI adoption and literacy](/context/sector/ai-adoption-and-literacy.md): when a plan assumes staff already use AI widely and well, leaves adoption, training and shadow use unowned, or depends on how people read, question, feed and set up AI assistants.
- [AI systems and governance](/context/sector/ai-systems-and-governance.md): when a plan connects AI to internal data or archives, or builds a shared AI tool, and meets data protection, EU compliance, security review or staff fear of surveillance.
- [Culture and psychology](/context/sector/culture-and-psychology.md): when a change or experiment meets fear for jobs, a newsroom built for accuracy rather than error, or consensus-seeking that makes the safe choice the default.
- [Horizons, measurement and priorities](/context/sector/horizons-measurement-and-priorities.md): when long-horizon or radical work is judged on the same near-term KPIs and return as operations, or the question is how to measure and prioritise it.
- [Innovation process](/context/sector/innovation-process.md): when an idea or project lacks a route to decision, an owner or room to iterate, or the team is designing how ideas move from proposal to production.
- [Innovation structures](/context/sector/innovation-structures.md): when the organisation is choosing where innovation should sit, such as a lab, spin-off, cross-functional team or forum, or struggles to carry ideas into production.
- [Leadership and mandate](/context/sector/leadership-and-mandate.md): when long-horizon work needs leadership buy-in, a mandate or resources, or depends on a few individuals whose departure would take the capacity with them.
- [Organisational memory](/context/sector/organisational-memory.md): when a plan risks repeating earlier attempts, or the organisation wants to capture what it has learned in a shared knowledge base or 'organisation brain'.
- [Sector conditions](/context/sector/sector-conditions.md): when a plan rests on assumptions about distribution, legacy revenue, business models or audiences that the media sector's shifting conditions may no longer support.
- [Strategy, planning and practice](/context/sector/strategy-planning-and-practice.md): when strategy set at the top must reach operational levels, planning horizons are shrinking, or the team relies on strategy and sprint tools rather than futures practice.
